Viewers and players, with instant page turns.

**Files**
- `Browser-0.6.0.ipa`: the app, unsigned. Sign and install it with Signulous (install over 0.5.0 to keep everything).
- `BrowserProbe-0.6.0.ipa`: optional test copy that asks for the default-browser entitlement.

**New**
- **Images** open in a new viewer that pages through every image in the folder:
  - The next image appears instantly, with no slide animation.
  - Ways to turn pages: tap the left or right edge, the ‹ › buttons, swipe in any direction, or use the arrow keys, Space, Page Up and Page Down.
  - A strip of thumbnails jumps to any image, and a counter shows where you are.
  - Pinch to zoom, run a slideshow, share or delete.
  - Settings › Images and PDFs has "Page transition: Instant or Slide".
- **PDFs** turn pages the same instant way:
  - Jump to a page, browse thumbnails or the outline, and search.
  - Each PDF remembers its page.
  - Continuous scrolling is available too.
- **Videos** play in the system player, with picture in picture, AirPlay and speeds:
  - Double-tap the left or right side to skip 10 seconds; hold the right side to play at 2×.
  - The folder's next video plays after this one, and each video continues where you left it.
  - Playback can continue in the background (Settings › Video and Music).
- **Music:**
  - Play a song or a whole folder, with shuffle and repeat.
  - Playlists, a mini player above the page, and Lock Screen and Control Center controls.
- **Trim** a video and save it as a new file.

**Device checklist**
- [ ] Open a folder of images. Tap the right edge and press → on a keyboard: each next image appears with no animation.
- [ ] Open a PDF and turn pages by tapping the edges and with the arrow keys. Close it and open it again: it's on the same page.
- [ ] Play a downloaded video and start picture in picture. Double-tap to skip, hold the right side for 2×.
- [ ] Play a song and lock the iPad: the Lock Screen shows the song and its controls work.
- [ ] Make a playlist and add songs to it from the Files list.
- [ ] Trim a video and check that the new file plays.
