Browsing features, iPad polish, and Firefox Focus–style privacy and search.

**Files**
- `Browser-0.3.0.ipa`: the app, unsigned. Sign and install it with Signulous (install over 0.2.0 to keep your tabs).
- `BrowserProbe-0.3.0.ipa`: optional test copy that asks for the default-browser entitlement.

**New**
- New Tab page with Shortcuts and Recently Visited. History (⌘Y), Bookmarks (⌥⌘B) and Reopen Closed Tab (⇧⌘T).
- Address bar:
  - Suggestions from history, bookmarks and open tabs ("Switch to Tab"), plus Paste and Go.
  - Inline completion of site names.
  - Search terms stay in the bar on a results page.
  - "Find … on Page".
- Search engines: Google, DuckDuckGo, Amazon, Wikipedia, Bing, Brave, Ecosia, Startpage and YouTube, or add your own with `%s`. Search suggestions are off until you say yes.
- Page settings ("aA"):
  - Zoom, remembered per site (⌘+, ⌘−, ⌘0).
  - Desktop or mobile site, and a user agent per site.
- Long-press menus:
  - Links: new tab, background tab, private tab, new window, copy, share, add to Shortcuts.
  - Images: save to Photos or Downloads, copy, share.
- A speaker on tabs that play sound; tap it to mute.
- iPad:
  - Tab groups in the sidebar and multiple windows (drag a tab out).
  - Full menu bar and keyboard shortcuts.
  - Narrow windows get a bottom toolbar.
- Tracking protection with the same block lists as Firefox Focus:
  - A shield shows what was blocked on the page.
  - Turn protection off per site.
  - A running total of blocked trackers.
- Private tabs: the trash button (⇧⌘⌫) erases everything, with Focus's animation. Normal tabs get Clear Browsing Data.
- App lock with Face ID (the whole app or only private tabs), and a cover in the app switcher.
- Settings: appearance, website layout, page zoom, user agent, search, privacy and security.

**Device checklist**
- [ ] Install over 0.2.0 with Signulous: your open tabs are still there.
- [ ] Open a news site and tap the shield: it lists blocked trackers.
- [ ] Private tab (⇧⌘N): browse, then tap the trash. The private tabs close and "Browsing history cleared" appears.
- [ ] Settings › Security › Face ID for the whole app. Leave the app and come back: Face ID is asked for, and the app switcher shows the cover.
- [ ] Hardware keyboard:
  - ⌘T, ⌘W, ⌘L, ⌘1–9, ⌃Tab and ⌘+/⌘− work.
  - They also work while typing in the address bar.
  - After a page loads, Space and the arrow keys scroll it.
- [ ] Create a tab group in the sidebar and move a tab into it. Drag a tab out to open a second window.
- [ ] Long-press a link and an image on a real site.
- [ ] Play a video or song: the tab shows a speaker, and tapping it mutes.
- [ ] Type words in the address bar to search. In Settings › Search, add your own search engine.
