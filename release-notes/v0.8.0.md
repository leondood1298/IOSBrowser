Saves streaming video (HLS) as MP4 files.

**Files**
- `Browser-0.8.0.ipa`: the app, unsigned. Sign and install it with Signulous (install over 0.7.0 to keep everything).
- `BrowserProbe-0.8.0.ipa`: optional test copy that asks for the default-browser entitlement.

**New**
- **Download** on a streaming video (HLS, the `.m3u8` kind) in the media list, or Download Video on a long-pressed one, now saves it:
  - When the stream comes in several qualities, you choose one.
  - The pieces download four at a time with the page's cookies, and encrypted streams (AES-128) are decrypted.
  - Then a short **Processing** step puts them together into one MP4 that plays anywhere, without re-encoding. Separate audio tracks are put in too.
- Pause keeps what's downloaded; Resume and Retry fetch only the rest, also after the app was closed.
- Stream downloads show their progress in the Downloads list and in the Live Activity.
- **Downloads at Once** (Settings › Downloads, 3 by default): when that many are running, new ones wait their turn as "Waiting…" and start oldest first.

**Can't be saved**
- Live streams, and copy-protected (DRM) streams such as most paid services.

**Device checklist**
- [ ] Open a page with an HLS video (for example a news site's video player). Tap the media button and Download. Choose a quality if asked.
- [ ] Watch it go through "parts" and "Processing" in Downloads, then play the MP4 from Files.
- [ ] Pause a long stream download, close the app, open it again and resume: it continues where it stopped.
- [ ] Start a stream download and go to the Home Screen: the Live Activity shows its progress.
- [ ] Set Downloads at Once to 1, start two large downloads: the second waits, then starts when the first finishes.
