Finds the video and audio on web pages, so you can save or play them.

**Files**
- `Browser-0.7.0.ipa`: the app, unsigned. Sign and install it with Signulous (install over 0.6.0 to keep everything).
- `BrowserProbe-0.7.0.ipa`: optional test copy that asks for the default-browser entitlement.

**New**
- When a page has video or audio, a **media button** appears in the address bar: a download arrow when something can be saved, a film strip when it can only be watched.
- The button opens a list of what the page has, with type, quality (such as 1080p), size and length:
  - **Download** saves it with the page's cookies, so files that need you to be signed in work too.
  - **Play** opens it in the app's player.
  - **Copy Address** copies its link.
- Long-press a video for **Download Video**, **Play in Player** and **Copy Video Address**.
- Video that streams in small pieces (as on YouTube) or is copy-protected is listed with the reason it can't be saved.
- Streaming video (HLS) is listed too; saving it arrives in the next version.

**Device checklist**
- [ ] Open a page with a plain video (an MP4). The media button appears; Download saves the video to Videos.
- [ ] Long-press the video and choose Download Video.
- [ ] Play a video from the list in the app's player.
- [ ] Open a YouTube video: the list says it can't be saved.
